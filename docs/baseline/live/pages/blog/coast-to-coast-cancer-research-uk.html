Coast to Coast - Cancer Research UK

Personal

Charity Cycle Ride to raise money for cancer research UK.

Ste Pammenter // @SvenPam // 16-Jun-14

Old Article

This content may be out of date or broken, please take care.

Last year I did the Cleveland Way and raised £310 between two of us.

This year I will be joining my best mates: Carl Morphet, Dan Holden and Dan's cousin, Alan Holden in a three-day 170 mile trip across the breadth of the England to try and raise £500 for Cancer Research UK.

We will be setting off this Friday, riding the way of  the roses from Morcambe, Lancashire to Bridlington, Yorkshire. The first day will take us on a 60mile ride into Pateley Bridge where we will stay the night.The second day we aim to reach Pocklington - 60miles -  to refresh ourselves for a third and final 50 mile ride into Bridlington on the Sunday.

We have chosen Cancer Research, because we have all been directly or indirectly affected by this ravaging disease. Only two years past my own mother defeated breast cancer, a victory aided by the work of cancer research.

I will be attempting to blog at the end of each day, so everyone can get up to date information on our whereabouts. Last year, we almost gave up on the Cleveland Way as our endurance hit a cliff - it was through your continued donations that we battled on. We would love to see the support again this year.
